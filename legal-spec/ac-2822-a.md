# AC 2822-A — implementation note

Baseline selected by the simulator:

- id: `ac-2822-a-2026-07-16`
- status: proposal
- source date: 2026-07-16
- stage: testo approvato dalla Camera, in attesa dell'esame del Senato

Primary sources checked:

- Camera thematic page, "Legge elettorale", updated for the 16 July 2026
  approval of A.C. 2822-A and transmission to the Senate.
- Camera/Senate Studies dossier AC0469c, "Disposizioni in materia di elezioni
  della Camera dei Deputati e del Senato della Repubblica - Testo a fronte",
  7 July 2026.

The current software implements these rule families:

1. input validation;
2. exact vote aggregation;
3. joint bonus check between Camera and Senato;
4. access thresholds;
5. national Hare quotient allocation by largest remainders;
6. bonus seats and maximum winner caps;
7. population-derived Camera/Senate territorial allocation of bonus seats;
8. Camera allocation to constituencies and plurinominal districts;
9. Senate allocation by region and plurinominal districts;
10. special uninominal territories supplied in the input;
11. candidate proclamation from ordered lists;
12. trace output for each calculation stage.

The foreign constituency is represented in the data model but excluded from the
domestic allocation pools. The full substitute cascade still requires official
examples before it should be treated as conformance-complete.

## Known rules represented

### Premio

The same list or coalition must be first in both chambers and must reach at
least 42 percent in both chambers. The first implemented API reports failed
conditions instead of silently applying a partial result.

When the premio is assigned:

- Camera: 70 seats are assigned to the winning list/coalition, with a maximum
  total of 220 deputies for that winner.
- Senato: 35 seats are assigned to the winning list/coalition, with a maximum
  total of 113 senators for that winner.

The ordinary domestic pools exclude Estero and the separately regulated special
territories: 384 Camera seats and 189 Senate seats. When the bonus is awarded,
the ordinary pools fall to 314 and 154 seats respectively.

### Ripartizione territoriale del premio

The 70 Camera and 35 Senate bonus seats are allocated by legal population using
the natural quotient and largest remainders. Valle d'Aosta and
Trentino-Alto Adige are excluded from both bonus pools. Exact boundary ties are
reported for manual legal resolution and the engine does not invent a
deterministic substitute rule.

Population is a versioned electoral dataset. Missing `electionDate` values use
the law source date, `2026-07-16`, so existing fixtures remain deterministic.
The `electoral-population-2026` registry entry is intentionally inactive until a
DPR date and effective date are configured.

The 2022 general election used the 2011 census under the law then in force. The
2021 tables in this repository are prospective AC 2822-A fixtures and should
not be read as a correction of the 2022 official allocation.

### Soglie

The engine applies:

- 10 percent for coalitions;
- 3 percent for lists;
- 20 percent regional exceptions;
- protected linguistic-minority regional 20 percent support;
- recovery, inside each admitted coalition, of the strongest list otherwise not
  admitted under the internal coalition threshold.

### Ordinary national allocation

For the first vertical slice, admitted subjects are allocated seats with:

- Hare quotient: valid votes / seats;
- integer seats: floor(subject votes / quotient);
- remaining seats: largest remainders;
- tie handling: equal remainders are reported as tie-resolution requirements.

### Ripartizione territoriale

Camera:

- national allocation among admitted coalitions/single lists;
- distribution to constituencies from ordinary input seat tables;
- distribution to plurinominal districts;
- compensation to preserve national subject totals.

Senato:

- regional allocation from input regional/district seat tables;
- distribution to plurinominal districts.

### Regioni speciali

Valle d'Aosta/Vallée d'Aoste and Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol are modeled as
special single-member districts when supplied in the scenario input. The engine
assigns the seat to the highest-vote candidate and reports an unresolved tie
when candidate votes are equal.

### Candidati

Ordered candidate nominations are used to proclaim elected candidates for
territorial list seats. Multiple nomination and substitute cascades are surfaced
in result data and trace, with unresolved cases reported as ties/manual
resolution requirements when the provided input is insufficient.
