# 2022 elected references

`elected-2022.json` is a local regression fixture derived from the Ministry of the Interior open-data CSVs listed in the file metadata.

It includes:
- plurinominal candidates marked with `CODTIPOELETTO=E` in the Camera and Senato candidate-list CSVs;
- single-member winners reconstructed from candidate vote totals in the Camera and Senato municipality-level scrutiny CSVs.

The fixture intentionally records the source coverage as imported data. The bundled Italy-level Senate scrutiny CSV excludes Valle d'Aosta and Trentino-Alto Adige, and the bundled Camera scrutiny CSV excludes Valle d'Aosta.

`elected-2022-full.json` is the full 600-member check fixture. It is generated by `scripts/build-2022-full-reference.mjs` from `elected-2022.json`, the local Estero fixture, and public post-election sources for records missing from the local Italy CSV coverage:

- 12 Estero seats;
- 1 Camera Valle d'Aosta single-member seat;
- 7 Senate special-territory single-member seats: Valle d'Aosta and Trentino-Alto Adige/Suedtirol;
- 1 Camera proportional M5S recovery assigned after the Court of Cassation resolved candidate-shortage/pluricandidacy cases.

This reference is an oracle for tests and diagnostics only. The simulator must compute from votes and candidate lists, not read this file while allocating seats.
